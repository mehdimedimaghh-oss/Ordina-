# Comande — short guide

## For the waiters

**Start of the shift**
1. Open **Comande** from the home screen.
2. Tap **your name**, type your **4-number PIN**. Your orders are saved with your name.
3. Another waiter on the same tablet? Tap the **name at the top** (gold, next to *COMANDE*) → choose the name → PIN. That's the quick switch.
   Leaving the tablet? **☰ → Lock** — the next person must type their PIN.

**Language / Lingua**
- Tap **IT** or **EN** (top right on the tablet, or **☰ → Lingua / Language** on the phone, or on the login screen).
- Each waiter keeps their own language: when you log in with your PIN, the app switches to your language by itself.
- The dish names don't change: Italian with English under it.

**Take an order**
- **Tablet:** the first screen is the **map of the 30 tables**. Tap a table. Menu on the **left**, the order on the **right**.
  Tap **‹ Tavoli** (top left) to go back to the map.
- **Phone:** tap **Tavolo ▾** to choose the table. Tap the dark bar at the bottom to see the order.
- Tap a dish to add it. Tap **−** on the dish to remove one.
  A black message appears at the bottom with **UNDO** — tap it if you made a mistake.
- Write the **client name** and the **room**. If you write a room, you **must** choose the building: **HCO** (Concordia) or **DEP** (Dépendance).
- **+ Note** on a dish: "no pepper", "well done"… The big box is a note for the whole table (allergies…).
- **Room service** tick = + € 10.
- **Served 0/2**: tap each time you bring one portion. When everything is served it becomes green **✓ Served**.

**Colors on the map and on dishes**
- Table **gold** = open order. **Orange border** = the table is waiting for **20 minutes or more**.
- Dish **green** = in stock · **orange** = almost finished · **red** = finished (ask the kitchen).

**End of the meal**
- Tap **Send to reception**. The order is saved in History and copied (you can paste it in a message).
- Tap **SHOW** on the black message (or **Reception view**) → the order in **big text** to show the receptionist.
- **The client pays at reception, not to you.**
- Sent by mistake? **History → Reopen** (only today's orders).

**Stock (Stock button)**
- In the morning: **Start new day count** (tap two times), then set the portions with **+ / −**.
- Every dish you add to an order takes one portion away by itself. "Stop counting" = this dish has no limit.

**No internet?** Keep working. The top shows **Offline · 3 waiting**. Do not log out. When the internet comes back, everything is sent by itself.

## For the owner

**Open the dashboard** from any computer or phone: open the website link → **Owner** → your email and password. (On a waiter device: **☰ → Owner dashboard**.)

**Dashboard**
- Choose **Day**, **Week** or **Month**, then tap a day in the **calendar**. Days with orders are gold and show the total.
- You see: **Total**, number of **tables**, **average table**, items and room services, **total per day**, **total per waiter**, **best-selling dishes**, and **every order** (time, table, waiter, client, HCO/DEP room, dishes, notes, total).
- **Export Excel** / **Export CSV**: the orders of the day/week/month you are looking at. The Excel file has two sheets: *Orders* and *Dishes*.
- **Delete** an order: tap Delete two times and write a reason. It disappears from the totals but a full copy stays in the **Audit log**. Waiters cannot delete.

**Menu & prices** — change a name or a price → **Save**. **Hide** = the dish is not on the menu anymore (old orders keep it). **Add** = new dish at the end of each section. All tablets and phones change at once.

**Waiters** — add a waiter (name + 4-number PIN), change a PIN, untick **Active** when someone leaves.

**Reception sheet** — every order sent to reception also appears as a row in a Google Sheet that reception keeps open (one tab per day, Excel download possible). Set it up once: see RECEPTION-SHEET.md.

**Import old history** — the file `history-export.json` from the old app (safe to do twice).

**Audit log** — who deleted, reopened or changed what, and when.

History is **never deleted automatically**. Waiters only see **today**; you see **everything**.
